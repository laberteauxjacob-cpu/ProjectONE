# Original Candidate05 sound sources

`Scripts/create_candidate05_audio.py` generates 48 mono, 48 kHz, 16-bit PCM WAV files using original mathematical sound design. It reuses Project ONE's own Candidate03/Candidate04 synthesis functions, with no external recording, sample pack, copied voice or Project Zero input. `manifest.json` records per-source hashes, seeds, duration, envelope, spectral-band measurements and the configurable group gains. WAVs contain PCM format/data chunks without author or machine-path metadata.

The bank contains two quiet continuous facility loops (ventilation and a distant motor), three water drips and three occasional pipe sounds; four each of infected breathing, pursuit, hit and death cues; six attack cues (two per swipe/rake/two-hand family); and six new shots for each of Last Word, Overcurrent and Gravebreaker. Infected cues use original turbulent/glottal excitation and formant resonators. Upgraded shots retain the physical report and mechanism at pitch 1 and add a separately designed higher-frequency energy component: violet ringing, short cyan discharge and ember crackle. Variants change the authored waveform, not just playback pitch.

`UONEZombieAudioComponent` owns two reusable spatial voices per infected. A shared world concurrency group admits at most four breathing voices and seven attack/hit/death voices. Breathing is staggered; pursuit intervals are 2.4–4.8 seconds, idle intervals 4–7.5 seconds. Minor-hit cues are throttled to 0.36 seconds, heavy-hit cues to 0.28 seconds, and the first 0.4 seconds of attack anticipation are protected from minor-hit replacement. Death stops living voices immediately and emits one death cue; destruction, player death and reset stop the owned components through their production lifecycle hooks. Concurrency may reject a requested cue; event counters alone are not audible-voice counts.

`UONEAmbientAudioComponent` owns two fixed spatial loops and two reusable sparse-event voices. The sparse group is bounded to three voices globally. Loops have low priority, localized water/pipe events are occasional, and infected action voices have higher priority than breathing/ambience. Actual audibility still depends on the final scene mix. The controller's pawn-proximity attenuation listener preserves the gameplay camera's spatial orientation.

Group CVars are `one.Audio.Weapons` (default 1), `one.Audio.Zombies` (0.8), and `one.Audio.Ambience` (0.55), each clamped to 0–2. Weapon per-definition volume and independent physical machine sounds retain their existing controls. These are development mix controls, not a settings menu.

Run `py -3 Scripts/create_candidate05_audio.py` to regenerate and `py -3 Scripts/create_candidate05_audio.py --check` to verify exact generated sources and metrics. Root-scheduled `Scripts/import_candidate05_audio.py` imports `/Game/ONE/Audio/Candidate05`; the two loop SoundWaves are marked looping. Source checks found 48 unique files, valid PCM and no full-scale samples. Waveform checks do not constitute listening, approval of realism, in-engine mix review or proof that every requested cue was heard.
