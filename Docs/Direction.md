# Project ONE: Containment / visual candidate 01

One compact underground containment hall. Response operative: graphite tactical layers, pale ceramic chest plate, restrained teal ID lights; recognizably human proportions and a purposeful two-handed carbine stance. Infected worker: faded ochre workwear, exposed pale skin, damaged left sleeve, forward posture, asymmetric but plausible gait. Environment: warm grey concrete, blue-grey machinery, ivory labels, amber safety marks. Matte surfaces, restrained specular and clear overhead illumination. No fog or darkness to conceal asset deficiencies.

Camera: elevated perspective, pitch -58 degrees; north-facing view with low near wall. A small number of silhouette-defining details must read at gameplay scale. Source rigs and baked skeletal clips are authored in Blender. Head and left shoulder seams are designed into infected mesh construction.

Scope: one player, one infected archetype, one carbine, one hall, rounds/points/health/ammunition, death/restart/pause, integrated blood and severing. Solo only. No progression beyond rounds.

Assumptions: Windows desktop, UE 5.7.2 already installed, 179cm adult player, centimetre world units. Entirely original meshes and animation. Native engine fonts and shading systems allowed; no externally sourced art/audio. Audio remains explicitly unfinished unless suitable original assets can be produced and verified.

Review standard: gameplay camera and motion, never a render alone. This is a candidate for art-direction review; visual approval stays with the user.
