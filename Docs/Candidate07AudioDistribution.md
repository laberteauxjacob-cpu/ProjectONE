# Candidate07 recorded-audio terms and credits

Candidate07 includes externally recorded sounds under their own CC0 and Creative Commons Attribution licenses. Those audio grants remain available independently of Project ONE's original code/art ownership and the separate Unreal Engine runtime terms. No broad license is assigned to the game by their inclusion.

The playable archive carries unchanged credits, source identities, edit recipes and legal notices under `ThirdPartyNotices/ProjectONE-Candidate07Audio/`. Start with `CREDITS.md`; the three Creative Commons legal texts and original contributor notices are in its `Notices/` subfolder. Bullet-shell contact includes pschneib's CC BY 3.0 source. Metal footsteps credit Eelke's original recording and congusbongus's edited files, retaining the original/current BY 4.0 and edited BY 3.0 chain. See the per-source inventory for every other contributor and recording.

The credits/recipes identify recorded firearm and object-Foley analogues and all edits. The source repository contains the retained originals, processed WAVs and editable processing script at `ArtSource/Audio/Candidate07` and `Scripts/process_candidate07_audio.py`. Repository locations mentioned inside those source documents refer to that source tree, not an omitted executable dependency. Raw source files do not need to accompany the playable package for the game to run.

Copyright and attribution information in the supplied source notices is preserved. Earlier Project ONE machine/pickup effects and the intentional upgrade energy layers remain original authored game audio. This notice grants no additional rights to unrelated source code, art or engine components. Technical audio verification is separate from perceptual listening and user approval.
